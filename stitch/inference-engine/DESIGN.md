## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a distinctive convergence between high-engagement consumer fashion platforms (such as Zigzag and Ably) and high-performance Edge AI engineering (NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX, ONNX Runtime inference, real-time pose estimation).

### Personality & Tone
- **Intimate & Chic:** Warm, welcoming, and intuitive; eliminating the sterile intimidation typically associated with hardware and robotics.
- **Computationally Transparent:** Grounded, crisp, and authoritative; proudly exposing latency SLAs (`< 2s`, `15 FPS`), neural vector embeddings, and runtime metrics as confidence badges rather than hiding them.
- **Editorial Soft-Minimalism:** Combining curated, high-end lifestyle fashion aesthetics with approachable pastel nuances and hyper-refined micro-interactions.

### Target Audience
Gen Z and millennial digital fashion enthusiasts, modern consumers who prioritize seamless lifestyle ergonomics, and technical stakeholders seeking tangible proof of on-device Edge AI performance in physical smart mirror hardware.

### Design Movement: Soft-Chic Neo-Brutalism & Ambient Glass
The visual grammar merges warm, tactile pastel surfaces with precise, high-contrast typography and pill-shaped status tokens. Crisp architectural cards leverage subtle, warm borders and soft ambient shadows, punctuated by expressive editorial headers and clean technical monospaced metadata.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic undertones with deep computational violets and ethereal lavender surfaces:

- **Primary (`#6050AF` - Deep Iris):** Anchor brand color used for dominant interactive triggers, focal vector tags, section accents, and active indicators.
- **Secondary (`#E6DEFF` - Soft Lavender Mist):** Generous pastel tint for feature highlight chips, status containers, hero tag surrounds, and low-contrast surface elevations.
- **Tertiary (`#2D264B` - Midnight Obsidian):** High-contrast grounding hue for primary action buttons, deep typography, and computational diagram nodes.
- **Neutral (`#FAF9F6` - Warm Pearl Canvas):** Warm off-white foundation that replaces harsh digital whites, evoking textile intimacy and living space mirrors.
- **Functional & Technical Accents:**
  - `tech-jetson-green` (`#76B900`) and `tech-onnx-blue` (`#005CED`) serve as micro-status dots indicating on-device Edge AI inference states and hardware runtime readiness without overpowering the pastel warmth.
  - `text-primary` (`#1A162B`) delivers WCAG AAA legibility against pastel containers while keeping a subtle chromatic connection to Iris.

## Typography

The typography couples high-fashion editorial presence with friendly utility and technical discipline:

- **Display & Section Titles:** `Playfair Display` provides refined editorial allure, evoking luxury fashion lookbooks and high-end mirror interfaces. Used for central narrative hooks and poetic taglines.
- **Interface & Content:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides high legibility for Korean and Latin body text, featuring gentle geometric curves that mirror modern fashion e-commerce apps.
- **Engineering SLA & Hardware Telemetry:** `Space Mono` is reserved strictly for Edge AI hardware metrics, model inference latencies (`< 2s`, `15 FPS`), clock timelines, and pipeline schematics (`ONNX Runtime · TensorRT`).

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1200px max container width with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and dynamic margin centering. Elements span fluidly in 3, 4, 6, or 12 column bands.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid columns with 20px gutters and 32px safe margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Single stack vertical flow with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) viewport outer margins.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Narrative sections use `space-3xl` (`6rem`) spacing for airy editorial breathing room.
- Modular capability cards and pipeline diagrams maintain compact `space-md` (`1rem`) to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) gaps, ensuring glanceable dashboard density suitable for mirror surfaces.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Lavender Layering** paired with **Ambient Frosted Glassmorphism**:

1. **Base Layer (Ground):** `#FAF9F6` (Warm Pearl Canvas) provides zero-elevation texture.
2. **Elevated Surface (Card / Module):** `#FFFFFF` framed by a 1px soft border in `#E5E1F0` and diffused ambient shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(96, 80, 175, 0.04)`.
3. **Interactive & Floating Overlay:** Frosted mirror panels with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, translucent tint `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, and a delicate highlight ring `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`.
4. **Hero Accent Depth:** Lavender containers (`#E6DEFF`) sit flush or lift smoothly on hover with a chromatic glow: `0 12px 32px rgba(96, 80, 175, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy adopts a refined dual strategy:
- **Structural Containers & Feature Cards:** Base radius of `16px` (`rounded-lg`) extending up to `24px` (`rounded-xl`) for main presentation units, producing a friendly yet structured silhouette.
- **Interactive Badges, Metric Chips & Pills:** Full pill contour (`9999px` / `rounded-full`) inspired by contemporary mobile fashion discovery platforms (Ably, Zigzag).
- **Embedded Hardware Media:** Rounded viewport windows (`20px`) with inset border strokes to emulate the curved bezels of bespoke smart mirror hardware.

## Components

### 1. Primary & Secondary Buttons
- **Primary Action (Brand Dark):** Background `#2D264B`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded pill (`9999px`), padding `14px 28px`. Subtle hover transform `translateY(-2px)` with shadow `0 8px 20px rgba(45, 38, 75, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary Action (Pastel Lavender):** Background `#E6DEFF`, text `#6050AF`, font weight 600, rounded pill, padding `12px 24px`. Transitions to `#D6C7FF` on hover.
- **Ghost Action:** Border 1px solid `#E5E1F0`, transparent background, text `#1A162B`.

### 2. Status Badges & Pill Chips
- **AI Eyebrow Badge:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), background `#E6DEFF`, text `#6050AF`, uppercase `label-caps` (`11px`), tracking `0.08em`, padding `6px 14px`. Often prefixed with spark icon (`auto_awesome`).
- **Hardware Telemetry Tag:** Background `#EFEEEB`, border 1px solid `#E5E1F0`, typography `label-tech`, text `#2D264B`, featuring a 6px status dot (`#76B900` for Jetson Orin active, `#005CED` for ONNX).
- **Filter & Silhouette Chips:** Pill outline with interactive state toggle. Unselected: `#FFFFFF` border `#E5E1F0`. Selected: `#6050AF` text `#FFFFFF`.

### 3. Feature & Inference Cards
- **Structure:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px solid `#E5E1F0` border, `20px` border radius, padding `24px`.
- **Card Header:** Eyebrow tag + Latin/Hangul compound header + icon indicator docked top-right.
- **Metric Footer:** Bottom dock with subtle `#FAF9F6` inset pill showing SLA targets (`< 2s`, `15 FPS`) in bold `Space Mono`.

### 4. Interactive Try-On & Look Comparison Cards
- **Dual Visual Windows:** Split cards with 16px radius, separating `Ambient Reflection` and `AI Fitting Layer`.
- **Floating Tag Dock:** Look tags (`TOP Ivory Knit`, `BOTTOM Charcoal Wide`) float over image assets via frosted glass badges (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).

### 5. Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Surface:** Rounded-full (`9999px`), background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px solid `#E5E1F0`, padding `14px 24px`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#6050AF` accompanied by a soft lilac focus ring: `0 0 0 4px rgba(230, 222, 255, 0.6)`. Placeholder color `#756F86`.
