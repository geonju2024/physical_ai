## Brand & Style

This design system captures an editorial fashion ethos inspired by modern Seoul and Tokyo luxury digital commerce platforms, high-fashion print layouts, and ambient personal AI. It blends tactile editorial sensitivity with hyper-clean futurism: the structured gravity of high-fashion curation juxtaposed against translucent, intelligent digital layers.

The brand persona is an articulate, culturally plugged-in wardrobe confidant. It speaks with quiet confidence, precision, and discerning taste. Interfaces must evoke the feeling of browsing an archival lookbook or an art-directed luxury lifestyle monograph, made responsive and contextual through ambient AI affordances.

The visual dialect utilizes:
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism:** Warm porcelain and milky-white backdrops eliminate sterile digital glare, grounding interfaces in the tactile world of heavy-stock uncoated paper.
- **Atmospheric Translucency:** Frosted glass membranes and soft ethereal lavender gradients indicate algorithmic intelligence without resorting to cliché sci-fi tropes.
- **Architectural Typography:** Deliberate tension between classical high-contrast serif display typography and geometric, structural sans-serif interfaces.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-refined fashion editorial hierarchy. The primary functional anchor is an uncompromising deep charcoal `#121212`, providing stark, graphic legibility against warm milky canvases (`#faf9f6` canvas base, `#f7f6f2` alternate surface). 

AI-driven interventions and predictive curation leverage an ethereal violet-lavender spectrum (`#9d8df1` paired with a luminous tint `#ede9fe`), suggesting intuitive intelligence. Subtle mood, lifestyle, and tactile accents introduce a couture blush rose (`#f472b6` with pale companion `#fdf2f8`), while polished chrome metallics (`#94a3b8`) bring balance to architectural lines and neutral counters.

Color application rules:
- Keep full-bleed backgrounds strictly within warm milky white and soft ivory tones; never deploy pure `#FFFFFF` across root layouts.
- Reserve `#121212` for editorial typography, dominant CTA buttons, and high-impact structural frames.
- Use ethereal lavender and blush rose sparingly as focal point tints, active status indicators, ambient gradient orbs, and AI recommendation tags.
- Chrome `#94a3b8` serves as quiet dividers, inactive control rings, and metadata accents.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a direct visual dialogue between runway catalog heritage and contemporary digital clarity. 

- **Display & Editorial Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Utilized for collection titles, curation themes, AI narrative intros, and lookbook section headers. Set with tight negative letter tracking and natural leading to create the visual density of high-fashion magazine covers.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Chosen for its clean, geometric anatomy softened by modern grotesque nuances. Maintains superior legibility across outfit spec sheets, technical metadata, price points, and chat interactions.
- **Micro-Copy & Category Tags:** Rendered in `label-uppercase` with generous letter spacing (`0.14em`), drawing from the catalog taxonomy of luxury houses and modern concept stores.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric 12-column editorial grid for desktop and tablet, collapsing into a balanced 4-column framework on mobile screens. 

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Employs generous margins (`margin: 2.5rem`), allowing whitespace to serve as an active luxury material. Lookbook galleries and outfit compositions break canonical grids intentionally using offset column spans (e.g., 5-column product imagery paired with 7-column narrative blocks).
- **Mobile (<768px):** Adapts to a tighter canvas margin (`1.25rem`) with seamless vertical scrolling, horizontal lookbook carousels, and thumb-accessible sticky docked bars.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Use `space-xl` between thematic editorial blocks to let imagery breathe. Maintain tight internal grouping within product info clusters via `space-xs` and `space-sm`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects heavy, artificial drop shadows in favor of layered material authenticity and ambient diffusion:

- **Tier 0 (Root Canvas):** Uncoated milky white surface (`#faf9f6`), completely matte without noise or reflections.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards):** Pure matte white (`#ffffff`) or ivory cards, framed by razor-thin translucent borders (`rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.06)` or `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.60)`). No baseline drop shadow.
- **Tier 2 (Ethereal Glassmorphism):** Translucent overlays for sticky headers, AI chat sheets, and outfit curation overlays. Constructed with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, a surface color of `rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.78)`, and an interior top rim highlight of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Drawers & Modals):** Soft ambient light diffusion tinted with atmospheric lavender: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(157, 141, 241, 0.12), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.05)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses deliberate, tailored geometry. Standard elements use moderate corner radii (`rounded-lg` at `1rem` and `rounded-xl` at `1.5rem`) rather than amorphous, overly rounded pill bubbles, retaining an architectural, fashion-first structure.

Tags, badges, and category chips adopt crisp, rectangular silhouettes with minimal 4px-6px corner softeners, mirroring bespoke garment labels and hanging price tags. Full circular geometry is restricted strictly to icon buttons, profile avatars, and swatch toggles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Editorial Button:** Solid `#121212` background, white text in `label-uppercase`, `0.75rem` vertical and `1.75rem` horizontal padding, border radius `0.75rem`. Hover state introduces a smooth, subtle lift and soft lavender glow (`rgba(157, 141, 241, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1px` border of `rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.20)`, deep charcoal typography. Hover shifts background to `rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.04)`.
- **AI Assist Action Button:** Gradient border or ethereal lavender wash (`#ede9fe`) with `#121212` text and an inline spark/stylist emblem.

### Chips & Fashion Tags
- **Curated Category & Filter Chips:** Crisp rectangular silhouette with subtle `0.375rem` rounding. Inactive: ivory background (`#f7f6f2`), subtle chrome border (`rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.4)`), `body-sm` typography. Active: `#121212` background with `#faf9f6` text.
- **AI Insight Badges:** Compact rectangular tags featuring faint blush (`#fdf2f8`) or lavender (`#ede9fe`) background fills with crisp tracking.

### Cards & Lookbook Tiles
- **Product & Outfit Canvas Cards:** Generous padding (`space-md`), crisp off-white surface, `1.25rem` corner radius, enveloped by a gossamer border (`border: 1px solid rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.06)`). Visual media uses `object-fit: cover` with subtle aspect-ratio framing (3:4 or 4:5 fashion portrait).
- **Frosted AI Recommendation Modules:** Glassmorphic card layered over lifestyle photography with `24px` backdrop blur, subtle inner stroke, and floating style notes.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Editorial Search & Text Inputs:** Warm ivory fill, `0.75rem` radius, flat resting state with a subtle border of `rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.12)`. On focus, transitions cleanly to a crisp `#121212` outline without fuzzy external drop shadows.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist square and circle selectors with a deep charcoal fill and white precision iconography when checked; unselected states use a muted chrome boundary ring.

### Curated Wardrobe & AI Stylist Modules
- **Wardrobe Rails:** Horizontal snap-scrolling rails featuring seamless edge masks and uppercase micro-labels indicating silhouette, fit, or occasion.
- **Stylist Interactive Panel:** Bottom-docked frosted glass conversation interface, enabling fluid style queries with prompt pills styled as delicate archival tags.
