## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a vibrant intersection of Seoul's Gen-Z hyper-curated fashion commerce and warm, domestic smart-hardware convenience. Moving decisively away from cold, blue-light cybernetic interfaces, the visual philosophy adopts a "K-Chic Home Living" spirit: warm, milky, tactile, and delightfully self-expressive.

The aesthetic bridges soft physical interior object aesthetics (porcelain ceramic coatings, milk glass surfaces) with dynamic street-style digital ephemera (holographic sheen, puffy sticker badges, candy-tinted micro-interactions). It speaks to fashion-conscious digital natives navigating wardrobe indecision by turning garment fitting into an engaging, gamified ritual of styling, snapping, and shopping. Visual layouts balance generous negative space with cheeky, buoyant micro-accents to evoke confidence, joy, and effortless trendiness.

## Colors

The palette avoids stark neutral blacks and sterile digital whites, grounding itself in cozy, humanistic tones with electric fashion bursts.

- **Primary (`#FF4B82`)**: Electric Bubblegum Pink. The core anchor used for primary calls to action, high-conversion fitting triggers, and live interactive state highlights.
- **Secondary (`#A78BFA`)**: Dreamy Lavender. Handles AI compute states, automated fabric analysis tags, and ambient virtual backdrop modes.
- **Tertiary (`#FF7052`)**: Juicy Coral. Applied to promotional callouts, live cart synchronized badges, and immediate user alerts.
- **Support Accents**:
  - Soft Butter Yellow (`#FEF08A` / deep text `#92400E`): Expressive rating tags, discount stickers, and mirror lighting temperature indicators.
  - Pastel Fresh Mint (`#34D399` / surface `#ECFDF5`): Body-fit match validation, size compatibility status, and checkout synchronicity.
- **Neutrals & Canvases**:
  - Primary Canvas: Milky Pearl (`#FFFDF8`) and Soft Blush Cream (`#FFF5F7`) for interior harmony.
  - Surface Containers: Pure Milk Glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).
  - Ink Charcoal (`#1E1A22`): Deep mulberry-tinted black for primary readability without harsh chromatic contrast.
  - Subdued Graphite (`#6B6472`): Secondary metadata, brand labels, and fabric specs.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes geometric warmth, structural balance, and approachable authority. It couples wide-aperture sans characters with tight letter tracking in display sizes to deliver the modern visual polish typical of premium Korean e-commerce apps like ZigZag, Ably, and Musinsa.

Body copy maintains high legibility across translucent mirror glass overlays and mobile screens through generous line height and medium base weights (`500`). Micro-labels and tag stickers use punchy extra-bold weights (`700` and `800`) to remain sharp when layered atop complex photography or ambient video feeds. Numeric indicators (fitting scores, price tags, body measurements) emphasize tight, proportional numerals.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an adaptable hybrid grid that transitions between the high-density browsing needs of mobile commerce and the clean, unobstructed viewport requirements of a full-length IoT smart mirror.

- **Mobile Companion App**: 4-column responsive grid with a standard `1.25rem` margin. Items tile into dual-column visual feeds mimicking vertical endless fashion scrolls.
- **Smart Mirror Display Interface**: 8-to-12 column lateral HUD layout. Center space remains clear as a high-visibility reflection zone, while interactive modules float docked to the left and right gutters (`1.5rem` to `2.5rem`).
- **Rhythm**: UI clusters use an 8pt architectural rhythm, with `space-sm` (`8px`) for intra-card content grouping and `space-xl` (`36px`) for section-level separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined "Milky Frosted Glass" treatment to feel modern, tactile, and non-intrusive inside a domestic living environment.

- **Level 1 (Subtle In-Flow Surface)**: Milky White fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`), subtle translucent border (`1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`), backdrop blur of `16px`. Supported by a gentle ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(255, 75, 130, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 26, 34, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover, Active Modals & Floating Pills)**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`, backdrop blur `24px`. Shadow: `0 14px 36px -6px rgba(255, 75, 130, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 26, 34, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Stickers & Holographic Badges)**: Crisp offset shadows with warm color dispersion: `0 8px 20px -2px rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.28)`.

## Shapes

The form language is deliberately bubbly, soft, and anti-angular. Rounded edges mitigate the hardware rigidity of a glass-and-aluminum mirror frame, welcoming hand touches, gesture waves, and handheld smartphone taps.

Interactive badges, status indicators, and micro-stickers adopt full-capsule pill radii (`9999px`). Content containers, lookbook panels, and fitting comparison viewports utilize extra-large soft curves (`20px` to `24px`), reinforcing the approachable, friendly character of the experience.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary CTA*: Pill-shaped with a vibrant gradient fill from Electric Pink (`#FF4B82`) to Juicy Coral (`#FF7052`), capped with a top internal highlight (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`) that gives buttons a juicy, candy-like tactile presence. Text is pure white with a `label-md` typographic weight.
  - *Secondary Actions*: Milky frosted pills with delicate lavender borders (`1.5px solid #E9D5FF`) and ink typography (`#1E1A22`).
- **Pastel Tag Pills & Badges**:
  - Fully rounded capsules (`padding: 6px 14px`) using soft pastel tinted surfaces:
    - *Live Fitting*: Lavender background (`#F3E8FF`), text in `#7E22CE`, prefixed with an iridescent dot icon.
    - *App Partner Linkage*: Butter Yellow (`#FEF08A`), text in `#854D0E` for badges showing retail integration (Musinsa, ZigZag, Ably).
    - *Match Metric*: Mint background (`#DCFCE7`), text in `#15803D` declaring size fit percentages (`99% Fit`).
- **Cards**:
  - *Product Cards*: 20px-radius milk-glass panels featuring edge-to-edge garment imagery, an overlaid favorite heart toggle, integrated partner app pill tags, and floating price points.
  - *Try-On Mirror Widget*: Floating overlay panels with frosted blur and high-contrast dual-silhouette comparison sliders.
- **Interactive Stickers**:
  - Playful badges rotated dynamically between `-3deg` and `+4deg` featuring candy drop shadows, sparkle emojis, and bold label copy to highlight real-time AI body scans and seasonal sales trends.
- **Inputs & Search**:
  - Pill-shaped search bars with soft ivory fills (`#FFFDF8`), inset shadows, bubblegum pink focus outlines, and trailing voice/camera search icon actions.
- **Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles**:
  - Squircle toggles and bouncy pill switches that transition with an elastic bounce curve when toggling between "Current Fit" and "Recommended Size".
